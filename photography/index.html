How it works

  1. Tell me about the shoot

    Date, location, how long you need me, and what the photographs are for — that last one changes how I shoot.

  2. Plan the details

    We agree the shot list, timings and anything that has to be captured no matter what.

  3. Shoot day

    I work to the schedule we agreed and stay out of the way of the event itself.

  4. Edit and deliver

    The selected images are edited and delivered digitally, in the formats we agreed.

Common questions

How much does a photographer cost in Accra?
Photography starts at $300 and I quote per shoot, because a two-hour portrait session and a full wedding day are not comparable. Tell me the date, location and how long you need me and I will send a figure.
Do you travel outside Accra for shoots?
I am based in Accra, and shoots elsewhere in Ghana may well be possible. Send me the date and location and I will confirm whether I can cover it and what that would involve.
How do I get the photographs?
Digitally, as a set of edited images. The selection, formats and delivery method are agreed before the shoot so you know exactly what you are getting.
Can you do product photography for an online store?
Yes, product photography is one of the services I offer. Consistency across a whole catalogue is usually what matters most for a store, so tell me how many products you have and how you plan to use the images, and I will quote for it.
How far ahead should I book?
The earlier the better for dated events like weddings, since I can only be in one place. For everything else, message me with your date and I will tell you straight away whether I am free.
