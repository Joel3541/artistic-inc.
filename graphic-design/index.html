Common questions

How much does a logo cost in Accra?
Graphic design work starts at $500, and I quote each project individually because a single logo and a full brand identity are very different jobs. Tell me what you need and I will give you a figure before any work starts.
Do you work with clients outside Ghana?
Yes. I am based in Accra and work remotely with clients anywhere — files, calls and feedback all happen online. Most of my communication happens over WhatsApp, Telegram or email.
What do you need from me to start?
A short description of your business, who your customers are, and any examples of work you like or dislike. If you have existing brand materials, send those too. We can talk it through over WhatsApp if writing it out is easier said than done.
Will I get the source files?
Yes. You receive the editable source files along with exports for print, web and social, so you are not locked into coming back to me for every small change.
How soon will you reply?
I reply to enquiries within 24 hours. For anything urgent, WhatsApp or Telegram reaches me faster than email.
