How it works

  1. Scope the project

    What the site or app has to do, who uses it, and what counts as success once it is live.

  2. Design in Figma

    Structure first, then interface — reviewed as a clickable prototype before anything is built.

  3. Build the front-end

    The approved design becomes responsive, accessible code that behaves on a phone as well as a laptop.

  4. Launch and hand over

    Getting the site live, with hosting, domain and code handover arranged the way we agreed at the start.

Common questions

How much does a website cost in Ghana?
Web and UI/UX work starts at $800 and is quoted per project, because a one-page site and a multi-page product are different amounts of work. Describe what you need and I will scope it properly before quoting.
Do you design and build, or only one of them?
Both. I design in Figma and build the front-end myself in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, which avoids the usual gap where a design gets approved and then quietly changes during the build.
Can you work on an existing website?
Yes — send me the URL and tell me what is not working, and I will come back on whether it is something I can improve and what it would involve.
Will the site work properly on phones?
Yes. Responsive design is core to how I build, so layouts are designed and checked at phone widths — which for most Ghanaian audiences is where the majority of traffic comes from.
Who owns the code and design files?
That is agreed as part of the quote rather than assumed. Raise it when you enquire and I will confirm the arrangement for code, design files and hosting before the project starts.
